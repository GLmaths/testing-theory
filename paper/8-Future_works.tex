\section{Future works}\label{sec:future}

The Harmony lemma is a first step towards a testing theory for lqCCS.
\ifshowappendix
\cref{app:smyth} gives a sound alternative characterisation of the Smyth
probabilistic must preorder; its completeness in general, and a
characterisation that does not depend on the particular shape of lqCCS but
holds for a class of calculi described axiomatically, are left for future
work. \cref{app:den} relates the reduction semantics to a denotational
semantics in the style of~\cite{FengXu23,UVY26}.
\else
The next step is an alternative characterisation of a probabilistic must
preorder on configurations, first for lqCCS and then for a class of calculi
described axiomatically, and a denotational semantics related to the
reduction semantics.
\fi
Finally, the memory is fixed here: the dynamic allocation of qubits is a
natural extension of the calculus.
