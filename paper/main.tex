\title{Harmony lemma for lqCCS}
\author{}
\date{}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
We present lqCCS, a quantum process calculus with asynchronous outputs,
guarded sums, tagged recursion and a shared quantum memory, in which every
nondeterministic choice is identified by the tags of the prefixes that
resolve it. Its labelled transition system on configurations is obtained as
the parallel composition of two simpler systems, the LTS of programs and the
LTS of the memory, where the memory has two kinds of actions only: the application of
a super-operator, which serves both the quantum gates and the initialisations
of the programs, and the measurements. We then define a structural congruence and a reduction
semantics indexed by the same choices, and prove the Harmony lemma: a
configuration reduces with a given choice exactly when it performs a silent
transition with that choice, up to structural congruence. All the results
are mechanised in Rocq, over an abstract quantum model.
\end{abstract}
